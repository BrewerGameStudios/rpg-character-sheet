How to Use the Sheet

  • Editing: Click on any field to enter character details.
  • Trial Access: During your first 30 days, you can use Save As New to create character slots. All other features require a license.
  • Flexibility: Although the sheet is strongly written around the 5e rule set, every field is customizable to fit those custom games.
  • Auto-Save: Your changes are saved locally to your hard drive.
  • Dark Mode: Use the toggle to switch between themes.
  • Install (Android): Tap the three dots (⋮) in Chrome and select "Install app" or "Add to Home screen".
  • Install (iOS/iPhone): Open in Safari (you must use Safari), tap the Share icon (square with arrow), scroll down and tap "Add to Home Screen" for best view use in horizontal mode as iOS isnt optimized yet.
  • Exporting: Click Export Character to create a backup file. These files are ultra-lightweight, making them easy to share with your DM or party via email, text, Discord, or any chat app that allows file sharing.
  • Importing: Use Import Character to load a file sent to you by another player or to restore a character from a backup file.
  • Cloud Saving: Click Cloud Backups to import, export, or remove saved characters from the cloud. Files are accessible by your unique user-Id only.
  • Your Email: We will never sell your email address. It's only ever used to send the occasional email when there's a major bug fix or update — and you can opt out of that anytime with the checkbox further down this menu.

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 16, 2026

Your privacy is highly important to us. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and how we protect your personal data when you purchase or use our TTRPG Character Sheet.

In short about your email: We will never sell your email address. It's only ever used to send the occasional email when there's a major bug fix or update.

1. Information We Collect

We collect minimal personal data to fulfill your order and provide product updates:

  • Character Data (Local): All character sheet data is saved directly to your device using your browser's local storage. This data never leaves your device unless you choose to use the optional Cloud Backup feature.
  • Cloud Backup Data: If you use the Cloud Backup feature, your character data and email address are stored securely via Supabase. This is used solely to provide save/load functionality across devices.
  • License Activation: When activating your license, your email address is used to verify ownership and manage device authorizations. It is not used for marketing purposes.
  • Customer Information: When you make a purchase, we collect your name and email address.
  • Payment Data: Payment processing is handled entirely by Stripe. We do not see, store, or have access to your raw credit card numbers or billing credentials. Stripe provides us with transaction confirmations and basic customer contact details required to deliver the Product.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information strictly for the following purposes:

  • To save your character sheet data locally on your device for seamless offline use.
  • To sync and restore your character data across devices via the optional Cloud Backup feature.
  • To verify your license and manage authorized devices.
  • To deliver the digital character sheet files and any automated purchase receipts.
  • To provide technical support or answer questions regarding your purchase.
  • To send critical product updates or bug fixes related to the character sheet.

3. Data Sharing and Third Parties

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. Your data is shared only with trusted service providers necessary to operate the app:

  • Stripe: For secure payment processing and automated transaction management.
  • Supabase: For optional cloud save and license verification. Only your email and character data are stored. No advertising networks or analytics trackers are used in this app.

4. Data Retention

We will retain your email and purchase history for as long as necessary to maintain your access to digital downloads, provide support, or comply with financial bookkeeping obligations.

5. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to access, correct, or request the deletion of the personal information we hold about you. You can opt out of email updates at any time yourself using the checkbox on this menu page — or, to update your contact information or for anything else, reach out to us at BrewerGameStudios@Gmail.com.

6. Security

We take reasonable technical and administrative measures to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, loss, or alteration. However, no transmission over the internet is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.
