SUBJECT: 🎲 Big Updates to Your Character Sheet — v2.2.0 & v2.3.0 Are Here!

---

Hey Adventurer,

It's been a while since our last update, and your character sheet has been busy leveling up behind the scenes. Here's everything new since v2.2.0 and v2.3.0 rolled out — nothing to install, nothing to configure. It's already live.

📝 CAMPAIGN & CHARACTER NOTES GOT A FULL REWORK
The Notes page now works a lot more like Google Docs. You get a visible cursor (finally), a formatting toolbar that stays put no matter how far you scroll, and a real "paper" page that grows as you write. Bold, italic, underline, bullet lists, and 9 text colors (plus a custom color picker) all now clearly show which one is active — and yes, notes now come with browser spell check turned on.

☁️ NOTES NOW ACTUALLY SYNC TO THE CLOUD
Here's the big one: Campaign Notes previously lived in their own little bubble and never made it into Cloud Backup or onto your other devices — even though everything else on the sheet did. That's fixed. Your notes now travel with the rest of your character everywhere: Save, autosave, Export, and Cloud Backup. If you had notes typed in before this update, they've been automatically recovered and folded into your character the next time you opened the app — nothing should have been lost.

⚔️ NEW ON THE SHEET
• Ki Points tracker, right in the Attacks box
• Bonus Actions box
• Casting Time / Duration fields on Spells
• A dedicated Cloak / Cape attire slot
• Recipe Cards in the Workshop — check off ingredients as you collect them, just like a real recipe card
• Swipe left/right on mobile to flip between tabs

🛠️ QUALITY OF LIFE
• Dexterity modifier now automatically drives Initiative
• Passive Wisdom calculates correctly (Wisdom mod + 10) and can be edited by hand for gear/homebrew bonuses
• Proficiency bonus is now editable
• New character stat boxes are flagged in red until you set them, so nothing gets mistaken for a real value
• The sheet now reopens your last active character automatically, with the save name already filled in — no more autosave silently failing because the Save box was empty

🐛 BUG FIXES
• Coins were rebuilt from the ground up: correct weight math, and coins can now be split across multiple stacks and containers
• Fixed autosave only triggering on typing — clicking Add/Remove buttons now saves too, and the app force-saves the instant it's backgrounded on mobile so you can't lose progress by switching apps
• Fixed potion weight totals not updating
• Fixed a frozen "Total Inventory Weight" display
• Fixed a duplicated Hit Points box and layout overflow on tablets/phones
• Fixed images failing to upload on iMac

🔒 A COUPLE HOUSEKEEPING NOTES
We refreshed our Privacy Policy and Terms of Service (both linked at the bottom of the sheet). Nothing scary — just want to say it plainly: we will never sell your email address. It's only ever used to send an email like this one, when there's a major bug fix or update worth telling you about.

That's the rundown! As always, if you run into anything weird, hit "Report a Bug" in the app or just reply to this email — we read every one.

May your rolls always be with advantage,
