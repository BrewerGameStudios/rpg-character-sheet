Big updates to your character sheet — v2.2.0 & v2.3.0
Hey Adventurer,

It's been a while since our last update, and your character sheet has been busy leveling up behind the scenes. Here's everything new since v2.2.0 and v2.3.0 rolled out — nothing to install, nothing to configure. It's already live.
📝 Campaign & Character Notes got a full rework
The Notes page now works a lot more like Google Docs. You get a visible cursor (finally), a formatting toolbar that stays put no matter how far you scroll, and a real "paper" page that grows as you write. Bold, italic, underline, bullet lists, and 9 text colors (plus a custom picker) now clearly show which one is active — and notes come with browser spell check turned on.
☁️ Notes now actually sync to the cloud. Campaign Notes previously lived in their own little bubble and never made it into Cloud Backup or onto your other devices — even though everything else on the sheet did. That's fixed. If you had notes typed in before this update, they've been automatically recovered — nothing should have been lost.
⚔️ New on the sheet
• Ki Points tracker, right in the Attacks box
• Bonus Actions box
• Casting Time / Duration fields on Spells
• A dedicated Cloak / Cape attire slot
• Recipe Cards in the Workshop — check off ingredients as you collect them
• Swipe left/right on mobile to flip between tabs
🛠️ Quality of life
• Dexterity modifier now automatically drives Initiative
• Passive Wisdom calculates correctly (Wis mod + 10) and can be edited by hand for gear/homebrew bonuses
• Proficiency bonus is now editable
• New character stat boxes are flagged in red until you set them, so nothing gets mistaken for a real value
• The sheet reopens your last active character automatically, with the save name already filled in
🐛 Bug fixes
• Coins rebuilt from the ground up: correct weight math, splittable across multiple stacks and containers
• Autosave now triggers on Add/Remove button clicks too (not just typing), and force-saves the instant the app is backgrounded on mobile
• Fixed potion weight totals not updating
• Fixed a frozen "Total Inventory Weight" display
• Fixed a duplicated Hit Points box and layout overflow on tablets/phones
• Fixed images failing to upload on iMac
🔒 A couple housekeeping notes
We refreshed our Privacy Policy and Terms of Service (both linked at the bottom of the sheet). Nothing scary — just want to say it plainly: we will never sell your email address. It's only ever used to send an email like this one, when there's a major bug fix or update worth telling you about.
That's the rundown! As always, if you run into anything weird, hit "Report a Bug" in the app or just reply to this email — we read every one.
